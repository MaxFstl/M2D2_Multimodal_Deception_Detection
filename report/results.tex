\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}
This section covers the the data preprocessing, model details and training, as well as the qualitative results achieved.
\subsection{Data Preprocessing}
Every video clip was cut into 64 frames. Afterwards the frame is cropped to the face and transformed to be $160x160$ pixels.

\subsection{Hyperparameter Optimisation}
For training the audio branch, we use the AdamW optimizer with a cross‑entropy loss. To find a good configuration on the DOLOS dataset, we perform hyperparameter search using Optuna over 20 trials. The search space includes:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Learning rate: log-uniform from \(5\times10^{-5}\) to \(3\times10^{-4}\)
    \item Batch size: 4 or 8
    \item Number of trainable encoder layers \(N\): integer from 2 to 6
    \item Adapter type: {\tt conv} or {\tt nlp}
\end{itemize}
For each trial, the model is trained for 10 epochs with a 90/10 training‑validation split. After the best hyperparameter combination is identified, a final training run of 50 epochs is performed on the full training set, and the validation metrics (accuracy, precision, recall, F1) are recorded. The resulting audio branch produces a 768‑dimensional feature vector per clip (obtained from the average of the last layer’s outputs), which is then passed to the later fusion module.

\subsection{Model Details and Training}
The model was trained for 15 epochs. We used a batchsize of 16, learning rate of 0.0001 and utilized early stopping to prevent overfitting.
To prevent overfitting further, weight decay and gradient clipping is used as a regularizer with a value of 0.01 and 1.0 respectively. 

\subsection{Results of the Main Model}
The model achieved an accuracy of 0.55, which is almost the accuracy of the original paper. With more computing power, a better
result can be expected. The confusion matrix (figure \ref{fig:conf}) shows, that the model can detect truth and lies evenly and 
does not collapse to only guessing one outcome.

\subsubsection{Text Part Results}

The notebook shows that the Whisper transcription pipeline, BERT-LoRA fine-tuning, model saving, and inference stages run successfully. The trained LoRA model is saved to \texttt{./bert\_lie\_truth\_lora}. In the recorded run, training completed after 400 steps with final training loss 0.170 and runtime 403.5 seconds. Only 296,450 parameters are trainable out of roughly 109.8 million total parameters, meaning the text branch trains about 0.27\% of the model parameters. Example inference outputs include both deceptive and truthful predictions. A remaining evaluation step is to run validation inference and the  accuracy is 0.5512, precision is 0.5495, recall is 0.99, and F1 is 0.70 on the held-out 15\% test split.
