\subsubsection{Text Part Results}

The notebook shows that the Whisper transcription pipeline, BERT-LoRA fine-tuning, model saving, and inference stages run successfully. The trained LoRA model is saved to \texttt{./bert\_lie\_truth\_lora}. In the recorded run, training completed after 400 steps with final training loss 0.170 and runtime 403.5 seconds. Only 296,450 parameters are trainable out of roughly 109.8 million total parameters, meaning the text branch trains about 0.27\% of the model parameters. Example inference outputs include both deceptive and truthful predictions. A remaining evaluation step is to run validation inference and the  accuracy is 0.6957, precision is 0.6667, recall, and F1 is 0.8 on the held-out 15\% test split.
