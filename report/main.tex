\begin{abstract}
Telling the truth from a lie is a difficult task. Generally, humans tend to change their behaviour in a slight way when lying. These microexpressions can be 
analyzed and can help to tell truth from deception. 
Multimodal Deception Detection (M2D2) is a system that uses visual, audio and text modalities to predict, whether a person is telling the truth or lying. 
Experimental results show that the model shows a general understanding of deception signs.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Author Contributions}
\textbf{Maximilian Foerstl} was responsible for the data processing pipeline. He implemented the automatic and dynamic
data downloading and the dynamic image and audio generation from the data. He ensured that data is not downloaded again, 
when rerunning the script. He was also responsible for the visual branch of the model. He also adjusted the fusion modules and
the adapters for handling the new text modality. He was also responsible for the testing and training of the main model. 

\textbf{Wenhui Dong} developed the text-branch project. This contribution includes Whisper-based audio transcription, preparation of transcript files for lie-versus-truth classification, BERT tokenization, LoRA fine-tuning of \texttt{bert-base-uncased}, model saving, and inference testing for the text modality.

\textbf{Tszhin Yuen} implemented the audio branch. Built the data loader for DOLOS audio clips, integrated Wav2Vec2 with frozen pre‑trained weights, and inserted two types of UT‑Adapters (convolutional and bottleneck) into the Transformer layers. Performed hyperparameter optimisation using Optuna and trained the audio model with cross‑entropy loss, recording validation metrics. The extracted 768‑D audio features are passed to the multimodal fusion module.
